# Mnemosyne

Problem - Ambiguous scientific questions and poorly defined delivery models make bioinformatics initiatives expensive before they become useful.

Solution - Mnemosyne Genomics is Stephen's independent advisory practice for organisations at the boundary between biological science, computation and product delivery. It clarifies the decision, evidence and operating constraints before selecting a repeatable delivery model.

Impact - The practice helps organisations frame analysis, technology and delivery choices with more precision. It is a compact expression of the portfolio's central idea: scientific capability comes from sound systems, clear communication and respect for the people who will use the result.
