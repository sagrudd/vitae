# Mnemosyne

Mnemosyne Genomics is Stephen's independent advisory practice for organisations working at the boundary between biological science, computation and product delivery. It frames difficult analysis problems before they become expensive delivery failures.

The work brings together genomics, bioinformatics and scientific software judgement: clarify the decision to be made, identify the evidence and operating constraints, then select a delivery model that makes the analysis repeatable.

It is a compact expression of the portfolio's central idea: scientific capability is created through sound systems, clear communication and an honest understanding of the people who will use the result.
