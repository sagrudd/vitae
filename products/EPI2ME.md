# EPI2ME

EPI2ME is the bioinformatics product ecosystem through which Oxford Nanopore customers turn sequencing output into accessible, reproducible analysis. Stephen's product leadership has connected scientific applications, customer evidence and engineering delivery into a coherent workflow strategy.

The product challenge is not simply to expose algorithms. It is to make a demanding computational task understandable at the laboratory bench while preserving the reproducibility expected by experienced bioinformaticians. That has meant treating workflow design, documentation, training and customer feedback as parts of one product system.

The portfolio case study centres on Nextflow-based workflows, cloud and on-device execution, customer enablement and the rapid COVID-19 bioinformatics response. The durable outcome is an operating model in which voice of customer informs roadmaps, applications scientists explain scientific context, and engineering teams can ship a usable platform.
