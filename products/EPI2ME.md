# EPI2ME

Problem - Nanopore users need analysis that is accessible at the laboratory bench without sacrificing the reproducibility and control expected by experienced bioinformaticians.

Solution - EPI2ME brings together Nextflow-based workflows, cloud and on-device execution, technical communication and training. Stephen connected scientific applications, customer evidence and engineering delivery into one workflow strategy.

Impact - Voice of customer informs roadmaps; applications scientists retain the scientific context; engineering teams have a usable platform direction. The same operating model supported rapid COVID-19 bioinformatics response and sustained customer enablement.
